# Product Hunt Launch Checklist

Use this checklist before, during, and after your Product Hunt launch.

## Pre-Launch (1-2 weeks before)

### Assets
- [ ] 6 screenshots captured at 1440x900 in PNG format
- [ ] First screenshot is the homepage (it becomes the thumbnail)
- [ ] All screenshots show real content (no loading skeletons or empty states)
- [ ] No cookie banners, chat widgets, or modals visible in screenshots
- [ ] Screenshots are in light mode (unless dark mode is the product's identity)
- [ ] Optional: screenshots annotated with captions or browser chrome mockups

### Copy
- [ ] Tagline is under 60 characters
- [ ] Description is under 260 characters
- [ ] Longer description covers: problem, solution, features, key numbers, tech stack
- [ ] Maker comment written in first person, casual tone
- [ ] 3-5 PH categories/topics selected
- [ ] All copy proofread for typos and grammar

### Product
- [ ] Site is deployed and accessible at the public URL
- [ ] All linked pages load correctly (no 404s, no broken images)
- [ ] Site performs well (test with Lighthouse — aim for 90+ performance score)
- [ ] Meta tags and OG images are set up (for social sharing from PH)
- [ ] Analytics tracking is in place (to measure PH traffic)
- [ ] Any "launched on Product Hunt" badge or banner is ready to add on launch day

### Account
- [ ] Product Hunt account is set up and verified
- [ ] Profile has a photo and bio
- [ ] You've been active on PH (upvoted/commented on other products) in the weeks before launch
- [ ] Hunter identified (if using one) — someone with followers to "hunt" your product

## Launch Day

### Timing
- [ ] Schedule launch for **12:01 AM PT** (Pacific Time)
- [ ] Launch on **Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday** (highest traffic days)
- [ ] Avoid major holidays, Apple events, or other high-profile launches

### First Hour
- [ ] Verify the listing is live and all assets display correctly
- [ ] Post the maker comment immediately
- [ ] Share the PH link on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and relevant communities
- [ ] Email your existing users / mailing list with the PH link
- [ ] DM close friends and colleagues — ask them to check it out (don't explicitly ask for upvotes, PH penalizes this)

### Throughout the Day
- [ ] Respond to every comment on your PH listing within 1 hour
- [ ] Share progress updates on social media
- [ ] Post in relevant Slack/Discord communities (where self-promo is allowed)
- [ ] Monitor your site's uptime and performance — PH traffic can spike

## Post-Launch

### Day 1-2
- [ ] Thank everyone who commented and upvoted
- [ ] Write a "lessons learned" post on Twitter/X or your blog
- [ ] Update your website with the PH badge if you ranked well
- [ ] Analyze traffic — where did visitors come from, what pages did they visit?

### Week 1
- [ ] Follow up with users who signed up during the launch
- [ ] Address any feedback from PH comments
- [ ] Plan your next launch or update based on feedback received
- [ ] Consider cross-posting to Indie Hackers, HackerNews Show, relevant subreddits

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Asking for upvotes directly | PH may penalize your ranking | Ask people to "check it out" instead |
| Launching on Monday or Friday | Lower traffic, fewer eyeballs | Launch Tue/Wed/Thu |
| Not responding to comments | Looks like you don't care | Set reminders, respond within 1 hour |
| Broken links in listing | Visitors bounce immediately | Test all links before launch |
| No analytics tracking | Can't measure PH impact | Set up tracking before launch day |
| Slow site under PH traffic | Visitors leave before it loads | Load test beforehand, use CDN |
